Questions about Resolve

What counts as "resolved"?

A conversation where the customer's request was completed by Resolve and the customer did not come back about the same issue within 72 hours. [Confirm the window before launch.]

Will it replace my support team?

No. It takes the repetitive work so your team handles the conversations that need a person.

Can it make mistakes?

Any AI can. That's why Resolve works inside your rules, verifies its actions and hands anything uncertain to a human.

How long does setup take?

Most stores connect in under an hour and start in draft mode the same day.
