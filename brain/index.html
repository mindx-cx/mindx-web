Find. MindX surfaces what needs attention, without being asked.

The moment your store connects, MindX looks for what's worth a minute: products selling less, orders running late, good customers going quiet, stock running low.

It keeps checking in the background. When something matters, it's waiting for you. On a quiet week, it says so and leaves you alone.

Act. See why it matters, then decide.

Every answer shows its numbers and where they came from. MindX suggests the next step and drafts it: a note to a customer, a reorder, a fix to a product page.

Nothing goes out until you approve it. Ask a follow-up in plain English whenever you want more.

What it keeps an eye on

Everything here comes from your Shopify data today. Support and marketing join as those tools connect to the Brain.

Sales and revenue

  • Revenue below a normal week
  • Products selling less
  • Discount codes giving away margin

Orders and delivery

  • Orders past their delivery window
  • Paid but not shipped
  • One carrier causing most delays

Customers

  • Good customers who went quiet
  • New regulars worth a thank-you
  • Customers with repeat returns

Products and stock

  • Stock running out before you can reorder
  • Products not moving
  • Return spikes on one product
